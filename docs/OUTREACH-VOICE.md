# Wulff Digital outreach voice

Brandon's direction from 30 October 2026 is a warm, personal and confident sales approach. The example is a friendly cold call in email form: show the impressive website concept already made for that business, sell the useful business outcomes and explain how easily the finished version can be tailored around them.

Lead with a truthful, specific research observation about their services or how someone can enquire. Keep website-absence claims bounded to the searches actually recorded. Do not invent a Google Business Profile observation, a booking attempt or a personal customer story.

Every draft needs the exact individual preview URL. Explain why the concept suits the business, then offer to adapt the photos, branding, wording and promoted services. Say that the package and scope are flexible and the price will be agreed before the paid build. Offer to help with the details so the owner does not need to arrive with all the copy prepared.

Explain search expertise through the actual work offered: mobile performance, clear service and location content and relevant page titles that help Google understand the business. Keep this conversational. Do not claim guaranteed positions, secret algorithm knowledge, Google affiliation, proven client results or credentials without evidence. The unlisted concept itself is a review preview, not a published, indexed customer website.

Use one friendly direct call to action: open the preview and reply with a good time for a quick call. Avoid false urgency, invented social proof, generic hype and quoted fees in the initial email. Keep the illustrative-image disclosure brief and retain the concise unsubscribe footer.
