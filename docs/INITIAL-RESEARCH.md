# Initial Wulff Studio prospect research

Reviewed **26 October 2026** using this ChatGPT session's web search and page reader. Four real businesses were identified and their own websites opened. This was a one-off research task; it does **not** establish an ongoing Brave Search connection or a deployed Atlas worker.

These are discovery candidates, not qualified sales leads, customers or evidence that anyone wants a new website. Facts below describe content observed on the official pages. Website problems, conversion rates, current supplier relationships, budgets and purchase intent have not been independently established. The page reader may use recently crawled content; a fresh browser check is required before presenting any defect as confirmed.

## 1. Cranbourne Fencing — Cranbourne

**Official website:** [cranbournefencing.au](https://www.cranbournefencing.au/)

**Observed 26 October:** The site identifies the business as Cranbourne Fencing Pty Ltd, gives a Cranbourne street address, and describes commercial, industrial and rural fencing and gate work. Its extracted homepage text includes repeated slider template captions, including “Slide title” and “Write your caption here”. This establishes what the page reader returned, not whether those captions are visible to visitors or affect enquiries. [Source: official homepage](https://www.cranbournefencing.au/)

**Opportunity hypothesis — unverified:** If a fresh browser review reproduces visible template content, a small content/accessibility cleanup and clearer service-to-enquiry paths could be relevant. A complete rebuild is not justified by this evidence.

**Next read-only verification:** View the homepage at phone and laptop widths. Inspect whether template captions are visible, present in accessible names or merely hidden theme content. Follow existing service/contact links without submitting a form; document any reproducible issue with its URL and screenshot. Only then consider a narrowly scoped recommendation.

**Disposition:** First candidate for an evidence-gathering browser audit; no contact or quote prepared.

## 2. Personal Performance Training Centre — Berwick

**Official website:** [personalperformancetc.com](https://www.personalperformancetc.com/)

**Observed 26 October:** The homepage places the training centre in Berwick, promotes a two-week trial, describes boxing, kickboxing and fitness training, and explains that classes can be booked through its app or a linked web booking form. It also promotes an online boxing programme. An existing signup and booking journey is already advertised. [Source: official homepage](https://www.personalperformancetc.com/)

**Opportunity hypothesis — unverified:** An audit of the handoff between trial signup, app installation and web booking might identify opportunities to make starting easier. There is no evidence here that bookings fail, the site needs replacing or a new integration is required.

**Next read-only verification:** Follow the trial and web-booking links on mobile and desktop without creating an account or reserving a class. Record destinations, login requirements, visible explanations and any broken link that can actually be reproduced. Ask for owner-authorised analytics before making conversion claims.

**Disposition:** Good match for Wulff Studio's fitness-sector understanding; commercial interest and any prior relationship remain unknown.

## 3. M&M Mobile Tyre and Auto — Pakenham

**Official website:** [mmmobiletyres.com.au](https://www.mmmobiletyres.com.au/)

**Observed 26 October:** The homepage advertises mobile tyre fitting and roadside assistance around Pakenham alongside scheduled mechanical services, including logbook servicing, batteries and brakes. It has separate service-page links and prominent telephone contact prompts. This is an existing service website, not a business without a web presence. [Source: official homepage](https://www.mmmobiletyres.com.au/)

**Opportunity hypothesis — unverified:** Emergency tyre enquiries and planned servicing need different information. Reviewing how visitors choose the right path could reveal a modest enquiry-form or service-routing improvement, if the current journey leaves a documented gap.

**Next read-only verification:** Inspect mobile navigation and the destinations of emergency and scheduled-service calls to action. Determine whether existing forms already collect service type, location and vehicle details. Do not phone, send a test enquiry or claim lost bookings without permission and evidence.

**Disposition:** Candidate for service-journey review; no demonstrated defect or demand for work yet.

## 4. Officer Plumbing Services — Officer

**Official website:** [officerplumbing.com.au](https://officerplumbing.com.au/) · [Contact and service area](https://officerplumbing.com.au/contact)

**Observed 26 October:** The official site lists domestic/commercial plumbing, emergency repairs, hot-water work and gas services. Its contact page says the business is based in Officer, serves Casey and Cardinia suburbs, and offers phone, email and a contact form. The page credits **Ascend Web Design**, indicating an existing website supplier attribution. This does not establish whether that supplier relationship is still active. [Sources: homepage](https://officerplumbing.com.au/), [official contact page](https://officerplumbing.com.au/contact)

**Opportunity hypothesis — unverified:** Optional enquiry triage or owner notification improvements may be relevant if the business identifies an operational need. Existing design credit and an established site make an unsolicited replacement pitch poorly supported.

**Next read-only verification:** Inspect the existing form without submission, distinguish urgent-call and non-urgent enquiry paths, and check whether any publicly described response expectations are clear. Establish the existing supplier arrangement and owner's needs before proposing work.

**Disposition:** Lower priority; respect the apparent incumbent and avoid assuming a redesign is wanted.

## What has actually been completed

Four sourced discovery records and specific next verification steps have been prepared. No businesses have been contacted, forms submitted, accounts created, calls placed, quotes generated, website defects reported as confirmed, or customers acquired. No paid research service was provisioned and no part of the A$300 budget was committed by this research task.

The most useful next step is the fresh read-only browser audit of Cranbourne Fencing's template captions, followed by the trial-to-booking journey at Personal Performance Training Centre. Keep every proposed improvement as a hypothesis until reproduced or supported by owner-authorised data.
