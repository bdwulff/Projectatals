## Batch 1 design constraints

### 1 Detailed ny red detailing

File: `01-detailed-ny-red-detailing.jpg`.

Black navigation above a saturated red hero with large rounded corners. Enormous white, condensed italic lettering overlaps a tightly cropped headlight photograph. The composition depends on the photograph’s crop, the size difference between the two headline lines, and the white capsule CTA. Translucent floating cards and a centred notch at the bottom of the hero complete the layout. Preserve these proportions; do not invent the displayed rating or product claims.

### 2 DriveClean dark detailing

File: `02-driveclean-lavender-dark-detailing.jpg`.

The lavender grid is a presentation board. The actual interface uses a nearly black garage photograph, restrained lavender-white typography, a fine header divider and a large circular downward arrow. Preserve the dark photographic mobile composition. Laptop, business-card and phone mockups demonstrate the design; they are not website sections to reproduce literally.

### 3 MOXOM yellow car and cyan panel

File: `03-moxom-yellow-car-cyan.jpg`.

Pale icy background with an extremely large condensed background wordmark, a central cyan vertical panel and a yellow car cutout layered over both. Compact navigation sits above; four numbered benefits form a disciplined row below. The cutout’s scale, overlap and negative space drive the hero. Adapt ticket and cart controls to the business’s actual enquiry flow rather than implying an operating checkout.

### 4 Next World VR editorial

File: `04-next-world-vr-orange-editorial.jpg`.

Large photographic hero with red and teal lighting, a white headline at the upper left, slim navigation and orange controls. Lower sections use white space, small side labels, tabs and image-and-text cards. Very large black section headings contrast with compact supporting text. Orange remains a controlled accent. Preserve the distinction between the actual page and the surrounding presentation-board panels.

### 5 Valora Heights alpine hospitality

File: `05-valora-heights-alpine-hospitality.jpg`.

Scenic blue mountain photography, elegant italic serif headings and charcoal mobile panels. Thin rules separate room, date and guest selectors; room photographs dominate detail views. Use the landscape and restrained accommodation interface as the reference, not a collage of four phone screens. Dates, room prices, availability and booking functionality must reflect real supported behaviour or clearly labelled demo interactions.

### 6 Hope Rise oversized typography

File: `06-hope-rise-cream-oversized-type.jpg`.

Cream background and exceptionally large, heavy black typography. A rounded portrait is integrated into the headline itself. Generous space and minimal navigation make one bright green CTA prominent. The bottom strip is quiet and structured. Preserve the typographic composition. Do not borrow charity endorsements, partner logos or other unsupported claims from the reference.

### 7 HomyClean cleaning grid

File: `07-homyclean-blue-cleaning-bento.jpg`.

White and pale-grey card grid, black capsule navigation and strong blue buttons. The hero shows cleaners at work, with a bottom gradient supporting the headline. Rounded service photographs, an asymmetric about section and a solid blue process section create varied page rhythm. Use this actual card-based page structure deliberately; do not turn unrelated references into the same grid. Testimonials, ratings and business statistics require verified evidence.

### 8 Still Home Care calm editorial

File: `08-still-home-care-calm-editorial.jpg`.

Airy white layout, dark navy text and a warm panoramic interior photograph. The large headline deliberately mixes sans-serif text with an elegant serif phrase. A floating white service selector overlaps the photograph; blue accents are sparse. Preserve the generous whitespace, type hierarchy and wide photographic proportions. Avoid filling the open space with extra cards or decorative effects.

### 9 EV mobile dashboard and map

File: `09-ev-mobile-dashboard-and-map.jpg`.

A mobile application reference: rounded metric cards, a vehicle cutout, restrained green status indicators, a dark map, pill navigation and a photographic station bottom sheet. Suitable for an application screen or relevant service finder; it does not define a generic business landing page. The screenshot supplies visual composition, not live vehicle, station or operational data.

### 10 Nexwash blue car editorial

File: `10-nexwash-blue-car-editorial.jpg`.

Pale blue-white canvas with a large blue vehicle cutout, visible floor shadow and generous space. The main headline sits above and to the right; a blue floating CTA card anchors the lower left. Lower sections alternate editorial photography, compact service cards and a dark contrast section. Preserve the cutout, clear hierarchy and asymmetric arrangement rather than replacing them with a dark full-screen background photograph.

### 11 DelishDrop food

File: `batch-02/08-puffhs-pistachio-pastry-olive-burgundy.jpg`.

File: `batch-02/10-the-nocturne-amber-restaurant.jpg`.

## Rebuild requirements

1. Assign each business one primary reference from the complete supplied set. Record that mapping before implementation, selecting a composition suited to the business.
2. Match the chosen reference’s composition, typography hierarchy, image crop or cutout treatment, spacing, colour proportions, corner shapes and navigation placement. Do not merge every reference into a common template.
3. Build real HTML and responsive controls. Presentation-board frames, device mockups and screenshots must not substitute for the actual interface.
4. Business research may supply real logos, photographs, services, contact details and location. It is not permission to introduce another design source. Use suitable licensed placeholder imagery where business assets are unavailable, identify it honestly, and treat proposed logos as proposed branding.
5. Do not copy unsupported review counts, customer totals, ratings, partners, years in business, performance figures or other claims from a template. Do not invent business statistics to fill the layout.
6. Static screenshots establish appearance, not proven animations or working integrations. Any motion must preserve the supplied composition and respect reduced-motion settings. Do not claim that screenshots demonstrate video, parallax or other behaviour that cannot be observed.
7. Verify the selected composition on desktop and phone, including the hierarchy, imagery, readable copy, useful navigation, keyboard operation and layout without horizontal overflow. A technical pass alone does not establish visual fidelity to the chosen reference.
